# Visibility Campaigns — Launch & Adoption Plan

Nov 22, 2026 · @Someone

## What we sell and the rules we've locked

Visibility Campaigns sells paid word of mouth that is measured: verified local creators post about a business in their own voice, the business pays only for approved posts and real views, and gets a report of what happened. We never promise leads or sales; we guarantee the posts and measure views, clicks, WhatsApp chats and promo-code uses.

**One line:** Ads reach strangers. We get your business talked about by the people your customers already follow, and we prove it.

| Rule | Decision for launch |
| --- | --- |
| Platform fee | 10% added on top of the brand's budget (₦15,000 budget → brand pays ₦16,500) |
| Creator fee | None. Creators keep 100% of what they earn |
| Minimum budget | ₦15,000 for open and verified campaigns at launch; review after the first 20 campaigns |
| How the budget is spent (verified) | 30% pays per post, 70% pays per performance (views) |
| How the budget is spent (open) | 100% per performance; no per-post pay |
| Per-post rate | Brand sets it; floor ₦300 (≈ $0.20), suggested default ₦500 |
| Performance rate | Brand sets it per 1,000 views; floor ₦700 (≈ $0.50) |
| Min and max payout per post | Required on every campaign; a post earns nothing below the minimum and stops earning at the maximum |
| Post fee unlocks when | The post is approved and stays live 7 days. Verification already filters for reach, so no extra view floor |
| Campaign length | Brand sets an end date, 7 to 30 days; views count up to 14 days after each post |
| Unspent budget | Becomes campaign credit, or a refund on request |
| Creator selection (verified) | 24-hour join window, then picked by creator score; 20% of spots kept for newly verified creators; the rest go on standby |
| Verification, per platform | 1,000 followers and 5,000 views in the last 30 days. Founding creators: 500 followers and 2,500 views |
| Platforms | TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts |
| Starting market | Businesses in Uyo and Ikot Ekpene. Streamers and clipping come in phase 2 |

Exchange rate used: about ₦1,350 per dollar (official rate ₦1,332 on 9 October 2026, [Techeconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/dollar-to-naira-exchange-rate-today-october-12-2026)). Fees and floors are in naira, so the rate only matters when comparing with dollar platforms.

**Margin warning.** At ₦15,000 we keep ₦1,500 per campaign before card fees, so launch revenue comes from volume. Plan to raise the minimum, or the fee, once case studies exist.

Each pitch opens with the life the listener has after Visibility, not with how the app works. Use the 30-second version cold; use the 1-minute version when they lean in.

### Creator (30 seconds)

Come earn with us. On Visibility, local businesses pay you to post about them, in your own style. You earn per post plus for every real view, and you keep 100% of it. Not verified yet? ### Streamer or creator who wants clips (phase 2, 30 seconds)

Your best moments die on one page. Put up a budget and dozens of clippers cut and post them across TikTok, Reels and Shorts. You pay only for verified views, so every naira buys real reach.

### Investor (1 minute)

Nigeria's small businesses are locked out of digital advertising. Ads are priced in dollars, the naira has lost about two-thirds of its value since 2023, and most owners never learned Ads Manager. At the same time, millions of young Nigerians have audiences but no income from them, because platform payouts are either closed to Nigeria or out of reach for small creators.

Visibility Campaigns turns those audiences into a local advertising network. A business funds a campaign in naira. Verified creators post about it in their own voice. Views are checked through the platforms' own APIs, and creators are paid per post and per view. We take 10% on top of every campaign and creators keep all of theirs.

We win where national clipping platforms don't look: city by city, for small businesses that measure success in WhatsApp chats and walk-ins, with field agents who sign up the owners who will never open a web app. Every campaign adds to a record of which local creators actually move customers, and that data is our moat. The product is built. We launch in Uyo and Ikot Ekpene, and our next milestone is 10 paying businesses.

## Creator onboarding pipeline

A creator should go from first contact to first naira in under 7 days; every step below exists to shorten that.

1. **Find them.** Search TikTok and Instagram for Uyo, Ikot Ekpene, UNIUYO and AKS hashtags; ask every new creator for 3 friends who post.
2. **First message (DM or WhatsApp).** "Hi \[name\], I'm Peter from Visibility Campaigns in Uyo. Local businesses pay creators like you to post about them, per post plus per view, and you keep 100%. We're picking our first 100 founding creators at 500 followers. Want in? \[link\]"
3. **Sign up and join the WhatsApp Community.** Welcome message, rules, and the date of the next verification session.
4. **Verify, per platform.** Switch to a creator or professional account (needed for the APIs), then check 500 followers and 2,500 views in the last 30 days (founding) or 1,000 and 5,000 (standard).
5. **First campaign within 72 hours.** Not verified yet? Point them to an open campaign so they earn and build their profile.
6. **First payout, made public.** Ask permission to share a payout screenshot; it's the best recruiting post you have.
7. **Keep them.** Weekly leaderboard, monthly training, and first look at new campaigns for founding creators.

**Founding creator deal (first 100):** lower verification bar, first look at every campaign, a founding badge on their profile, and free training. In return: post their own content 3 times a week and keep campaign posts live for 30 days.

**Watch for:** bought followers, sudden view spikes and recycled posts. A creator caught faking views loses the spot and is not paid.

## Business sales pipeline

Sell in person first: a Uyo shop owner buys from a face, and the first 10 businesses come from walk-ins and agents, not ads.

1. **Target list.** Businesses already running promos: electronics and gadget shops, restaurants and caterers, salons, fashion, pharmacies, event organisers. Start with those that post on social media but get little engagement.
2. **Walk in with the price card** (one A5 page or WhatsApp image): what it is, ₦15,000 starting budget, what they get, one result.
3. **The 60-second conversation.** Ask: "How do people hear about your promo now?" Then: "What if 20 people your customers follow all posted about it this week, and you only paid for posts we checked and real views?"
4. **Set it up for them on the spot.** The agent or founder creates the campaign with the owner: goal, budget, assets, brief, end date. Payment by transfer or card.
5. **Updates on WhatsApp** while the campaign runs: posts going live, early views.
6. **Report and rebook.** Deliver the report in person or on WhatsApp within 2 days of the end date, and offer the next campaign with a bonus credit.

**Founding Brand offer (first 10 businesses):** no platform fee on their first campaign, a featured spot in our own posts, and first choice of creators. In return: a 10-minute feedback call and permission to publish their results as a case study.

## Platform to-dos and open questions

CAC registration and the API applications are the two items that set the launch date, so both start in week 1.

- [ ] Start CAC registration (Meta's app review usually needs a verified business)
- [ ] TikTok developer app with the Display API: user info, video list and video query give each video's views and engagement
- [ ] Meta app (Instagram API with Instagram Login, and Facebook Graph API video insights); creators must use creator, business or professional accounts
- [ ] YouTube Data API key for public Shorts view counts
- [ ] Until APIs are approved: post link plus screenshot, checked by the AI verifier and a person
- [ ] Update the site: add YouTube Shorts, the 10% fee, creators keep 100%, open vs verified campaigns, founding creator offer
- [ ] Build the campaign settings: per-post rate, rate per 1,000 views, min and max payout, end date
- [ ] Build the 24-hour join window, creator-score selection, the 20% newcomer quota and the standby list
- [ ] Credit-or-refund flow for unspent budget
- [ ] Report template a business can read in 30 seconds

**Open questions**

- Is the launch minimum ₦15,000 for both campaign types, or ₦15,000 open and ₦30,000 verified?
- At a 10% fee, is ₦1,500 per minimum campaign enough while you're paying agents and card fees?
- How is creator score calculated: on-time posting, approval rate, average views, or all three?
- Do Founding Brands skip the fee on one campaign, or on their first three?
