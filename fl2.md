## Where to search, and what each licence allows

| Source | Licence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pexels**, **Unsplash**, **Pixabay**, **Mixkit**, **Coverr** | Free for commercial use, no credit needed | The people in them must never be shown endorsing a named brand. This is the rule we've been following |
| **Vecteezy**, **Freepik** | "Free" usually needs a credit line; Pro needs a paid plan | Check each file's licence before downloading |
| **Getty, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock** | Paid | Best African footage, and a paid licence often allows commercial "lifestyle" use. Worth it later for hero shots |
| **Nappy** (nappy.co) | Free | Specifically Black and brown people, good quality |
| **Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok** | **Not for use, inspiration only** | Fine for finding your taste, but those images belong to someone else |

**Search tips:**
- Add `vertical` or `portrait` for phone-shaped videos.
- Add `nigeria`, `lagos`, `ghana` or `africa` to any term.
- When one clip fits your taste, open the photographer's profile. Their other shots from the same shoot often match.
